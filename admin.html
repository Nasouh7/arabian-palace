The palace

A Yemeni kitchen, a Nairobi table

Arabian Palace cooks the food of the Arabian Peninsula the way it is cooked at home: mandi spiced and smoky, hanidh roasted until it falls from the bone, saltah brought to the table still bubbling. Platters are made for sharing.

Every table starts with a bowl of soup on the house. Come for breakfast from 8 am, a long dinner, or a late cup of karak tea. Everything we serve is 100% halal.

Good to know

Questions guests ask

Is all the food at Arabian Palace halal?

Yes. Every dish, sauce and drink we serve is halal. We serve no pork and no alcohol.

Where is Arabian Palace in Nairobi?

On Marcus Garvey Road, off Argwings Kodhek Road, in Kilimani, Nairobi. Tap “Open in Google Maps” above for directions.

What are your opening hours?

Every day from 8:00 am to 11:30 pm, breakfast included.

What should I order the first time?

Mandi (chicken or lamb), the mixed grill platter to share, saltah at breakfast and kunafa to finish.

Can I order a whole goat or food for a group?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 0797 666 667 at least a day ahead for a whole goat or large platters.

Can I order from my table?

Yes. Scan the QR code on your table, choose your language and send your order straight to the kitchen. You can call a waiter from your phone too.

Do your staff speak Arabic and Swahili?

Yes. Full service is available in Arabic, Swahili and English: our waiters, the phone and WhatsApp, the menu and ordering from your table.

Come hungry. Leave happy.

Walk in any day from 8 am, or message us to book a table, plan a gathering or order a whole goat a day ahead.
